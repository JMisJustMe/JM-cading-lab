WHAT YOU ARE PLAYING · RELAY FRONTIER

Drag backwards from the beacon on the left, then release to send a living pulse forward. Aim at incoming forms before they reach the beacon. Every contact must pass the bodies' route and state rules.

THREAD curves toward targets; PIERCE is faster and continues through enemies; BLOOM strikes neighbouring forms. Watch Manta wing-dives, Bramble splits, Weaver phasing, Darter lunges, Sentry attacks and the fourth-wave Guardian. Evolve between waves, intercept hostile bolts, use FIELD SLOW, earn an ECHO BURST through actual contacts, and deploy up to two relay allies to protect vulnerable lanes. Each wave changes the environment: VERDANT rewards clears, CROSSWIND bends shots, ION STORM speeds enemy bolts, and ECLIPSE strengthens enemy phasing. Relays fire traceable THREAD pulses but can be destroyed by hostile bolts.

Source bodies & proof boundary

Runs source-grammar interpreters based on the current-native JM Sovereign Batch Three: ContactBand (drag distance classified, not actual touch-force sensor); GlyphBody (shot selection); FormulaGate (hit permission); RouteFrame (success/deflection); StateField (shield → exposed → cleared). The local game loop, physics and renderer are new JavaScript carrier logic. This is not a claim of importing the entire Batch Three implementation or of independent native Android execution.

Body identity is preserved by separate source programs and receipts, not represented as five distinct operating-system processes. The game does not use Sovereign Ten RouteVM / JM GameCore. This v0.6 descendant inherits the frozen v0.1→v0.3 contact rules and adds a separate JS carrier for allied relay behaviour and changing field conditions. The inherited combat path remains source-aligned. The new CONDUCT path separately embeds the recovered original Batch Three JavaScript executors at commit b4c5cb7135c17be97c211c12d8b748e2bb0ca0d5, connected by a new adapter. This is original JS execution, not compiled Android/native-engine proof. Its six-body result also produces an SVG form card and PCM WAV motif. Living companions inherit committed pairs of original routes. Physical owner-device acceptance remains HOLD.
