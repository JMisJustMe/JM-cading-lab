Move with intent. Read incoming attacks. Perfect-dodge into a countershot.

📱 Dedicated FIRE: tap to shoot, hold for repeat. You can also drag your aim thumb and release to fire as before. Fullscreen is optional. Landscape is recommended.

Phone: plant your left and right thumbs anywhere in their lower-side control fields. Left steers with analogue speed and smooth corner gliding; right aims independently. Press FIRE to shoot or hold it for repeat. In HYBRID, you can also hold the aim thumb to fire, then release; in PRECISION, release fires one shot. Tap DODGE to evade or POWER for your fighter ability. Cards remain optional tactical actions. Land three hits within three seconds for your signature combo. Dodge into the path of an incoming shot at the last moment to earn a temporary COUNTERSHOT: Volt overcharges one bolt, Ember loads a boosted centre pellet, and Wraith steals health on a landed countershot. The AI reads projectile threats and cover instead of charging headfirst. Reinforced cover breaks and district pulses warn before striking. Switch reactive hazards off in settings if preferred.
Desktop: WASD + mouse cursor; left-click hold to fire; Space to shoot; E dodge; Q power; 1–4 cards. P2: arrows move; IJKL aim; Enter shoot; Shift power; 7, 8, 9, 0 cards. Gamepad P1: dual sticks, RT shoot, A dodge, RB power. P pauses, V switches cameras, M toggles sound. First to three eliminations or highest score at 3:00 wins. PvP is local same-keyboard, not online.
