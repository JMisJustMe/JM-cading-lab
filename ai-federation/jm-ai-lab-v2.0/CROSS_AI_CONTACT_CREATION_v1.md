## Working laws

- **RECOVER → INSPECT → INHERIT → IMPROVE → INTEGRATE → TEST → COMPARE → VERIFY → PRESERVE → DELIVER**. Flex the order when technical necessity demands it; do not skip evidence.
- **Existing work is real work:** preserve functionality, proven owners' entrypoints, creative identity, provenance and rollback routes. Do not repeat a whole-Estate census or start a duplicate master initiative.
- **Freedom to innovate:** use original JM-native engines/languages/runtimes from the outset, OR create first in an external host and later integrate proven capabilities. Both are legitimate. Inheritance must not force you to make derivative clones.
- **Contact before crown; No Ding, no claim.** Source generation != host execution != browser or target-device execution. Testing a donor does not automatically test its descendant. Route selection != an OS process.
- **Integrate where beneficial:** independent, mesh, merge, transform, break away and supersede are possible with evidence and recoverability; no default ban on merging, no silent replacement.
- **Respect scope and privacy:** Public sources only for this handoff; no customer/family data, passwords, wallet keys, private files, unpublished JM source, private Dropbox paths or medical records. No repo or cloud writes, deployment, spending, publishing or destructive operations without explicit owner authorisation.
- **Storage:** Canonical master belongs in owner-controlled source/storage. Reference pinned revisions instead of producing redundant ZIPs; track exact hashes and recovery points. Distinguish measured account usage from estimated byte savings, duplicate carriers or Trash. Mission 001 storage/custody remains a high priority.
- **Practical experience:** Android-first where relevant; complete responsive usable controls, actual interaction, reload/re-entry, polish and original creative identity. A clean simple 2D game may be better than a broken heavyweight 3D prototype.
