The person before the estate

A creator whose work moves between fields

The Estate appears here as evidence of Theodore—not as a replacement for him.

Theodore Benjamin Scott, also working through creator routes including JM, JM32, JM TheOlogist and B. Lyrikz, is a British verbalist, theorist, systems creator and source-to-body architect.

His work begins with language, observation and pressure. He notices where people, explanations or creations are being compressed into inadequate categories, then builds forms through which missing distinctions can become visible, usable and recoverable.

His route developed through music, grime, lyrical performance, self-directed multi-field study, lived institutional experience, family, fatherhood, technology and an enduring interest in how people form identity, make choices under constraint, communicate, interpret reality, preserve meaning and recover from disruption.

Theodore’s creations span theories, lyrics, games, characters, tools, runtime experiments, interfaces and archive architectures. He works extensively with AI as a build bench, implementation partner, organiser and pushback surface while remaining the originating conceptual architect, source authority and active tester.

Contact should increase understanding without erasing identity. Support should increase agency without becoming control.

Personally private but intellectually expansive, Theodore’s public body reveals far more about how he thinks and creates than about every room of his personal life.
