Long public biography

Theodore Benjamin Scott, also working through creator routes including JM, JM32, JM TheOlogist and B. Lyrikz, is a British verbalist, lyricist, theorist, systems creator and source-to-body architect. His work begins with language, observation and pressure. He notices where people, explanations or creations are being compressed into inadequate categories, then builds forms through which missing distinctions can become visible, usable and recoverable. His route developed through music, grime, lyrical performance, self-directed multi-field study, lived institutional experience, family, fatherhood, technology and an enduring interest in how people form identity, make choices under constraint, communicate, interpret reality, preserve meaning and recover from disruption. Theodore’s creations span theories, lyrics, games, characters, story worlds, apps, interfaces, coding bodies, proof methods and archive architectures. His systems repeatedly move from source to signal, contact, route, state change, trace, recovery and outward form. He works extensively with AI as a build bench, implementation collaborator, organiser and pushback surface while remaining the originating conceptual architect, source authority and active tester. Personally private but intellectually expansive, Theodore’s public body reveals far more about how he thinks, builds and corrects than about every room of his personal life. The JM Estate appears as evidence of Theodore; it does not replace him.
