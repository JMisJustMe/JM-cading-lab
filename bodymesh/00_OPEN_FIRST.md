## Purpose

BodyMesh is a separate backup and common-body build route. It does not replace JM Estate Compass, Estate Head, First Stage, Bringing It All Together, Theory Multihub, Library, Wisebase, GitHub, chats or Projects.

It receives those bodies as sources, keeps each source and identity traceable, finds tested common functions, groups related bodies without flattening them, and compiles additional descendants that must actually exist and work before they count as byproducts.
