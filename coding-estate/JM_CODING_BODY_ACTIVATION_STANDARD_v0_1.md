Every portable runtime session emits `JM.CodingBodyActivation/1.0` containing:

- surface;
- policy + Current-64 digests;
- 64/64 body-state accounting;
- exact active body IDs/names/reasons;
- exact available-but-not-selected IDs/names;
- adjacent supporting organs for the selected host surface;

The following invariant must hold:

`ACTIVE_ROUTE + AVAILABLE_NOT_SELECTED = 64`

No body may appear twice. No unknown body may enter the record.

## Surface support

Adjacent organs are recorded separately because:

**MEMBER OF X HOUSE ≠ IS AN X.**

For example, the Android carrier may use **Android Forge**, **JM Target Bridge**, **TraceBox** and recovery organs without silently promoting those organs into the Current-64 coding count.
