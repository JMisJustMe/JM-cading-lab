# JM LIVE DIRECT DOOR LAW v1.0

**Status:** ESTATE DELIVERY LAW  
**Authority:** Theodore Benjamin Scott / JM

## Law

For an estate-hosted web body, the owner's normal contact route is the **real live direct URL**.

1. Build from the authoritative source body.
2. Preserve or create one singular `OPEN_FIRST` / canonical front door.
3. Mount that door through the existing estate publication rail.
4. Prove the exact live route and its required assets before presenting it as live.
5. Hand the owner the **live direct door first, every time**.
6. Do not make a sandbox link, ZIP, duplicate launcher, or loose file collection the primary route when a proved live estate route exists.
7. Sandbox/package/download routes remain valid as fallbacks, archival bodies, offline transfer bodies, or when no live estate route can truthfully be produced.
8. A live HTTP contact Ding is not a Cold Ding. Owner-side physical contact remains its own proof stage.

## Short form

**SOURCE → OPEN_FIRST → ESTATE MOUNT → LIVE PROOF → DIRECT DOOR**

No fake door. No needless wrapper. No sandbox-first delivery when the estate can carry the body.
