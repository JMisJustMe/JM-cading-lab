# JM Original Intent — Individual Capability After Fusion

Status: GOVERNING CORRECTION

## What originally happened

The early coding estate did not begin as one generic language or one generic engine. The original intention was for the early coding bodies — including the Starter 13 lineage and Finger One / Finger Two — to become individually capable bodies.

Over time, those bodies were increasingly fused into shared houses, runtimes, registries, bridges, compilers, VMs, game cores and delivery systems. That fusion produced useful shared machinery, but it happened before every originating body had been matured to the same independent technical standard.

## Governing interpretation

The fusion is not to be undone or treated as waste.

It becomes the shared donor estate.

Each original coding body must now inherit from that donor estate while retaining or recovering its own:

- source authority;
- native syntax and semantics;
- parser / frontend;
- specialist IR;
- compiler and SDK namespace;
- runtime or engine behaviour;
- kernel personality and boot receipt where appropriate;
- tests, faults, recovery and trace identity;
- delivery and restoration route.

## Equality law

Equal standing does not mean identical implementation.

A language body must become a complete language body.
A compiler body must become a complete compiler body.
A VM or runtime body must become a complete execution body.
A container, house or registry must become a complete coordinating body.
A game engine must become a complete game engine.

No body is forced into a false role merely to satisfy a checklist.

## Kernel law

The EveryKernel lane remains valid:

- 100 separately identifiable kernel personalities;
- generated through matching body-authority routes;
- separate source, compiler, trace, fault and receipt identity;
- one container above them for registration, selection and federation;
- no container may replace the sovereign kernels beneath it.

Shared machine glue is permitted. Shared source authority is not.

## Recovery law

The correction is not "go backwards and erase what was built."

It is:

> Preserve the fused donor body. Restore the sovereign bodies. Raise each one to the strongest appropriate standard. Then let them cooperate without losing identity.

## Game-engine parallel

The same law applies to the game-engine estate.

Existing convergence engines remain shared donors, but GameForge, GlyphForge, JM GameCore, Kading Game Estate Engine, PLAYFORM, JM GAME NATIVE CORE and other distinct engines must each be raised independently to the strongest engine standard before federation.

## Claim boundary

This document records architectural intent and governs future implementation. It does not claim that every body or engine has already reached full native, machine-proven parity.

## Keeper lines

> Fusion created the factory. Recovery restores the workers.

> One estate does not require one body.

> Shared organs do not erase sovereign capability.

> It is not too late once the donor already exists; the donor is what makes the restoration possible.
