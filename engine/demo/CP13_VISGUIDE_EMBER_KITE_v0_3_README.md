## This revision uses four *real camera scene passes*
- **ACTOR:** only the playable animal actor on a neutral lighting/studio field; worlds/enemies and decorative scene elements are hidden. No composited image or static illustration.
- **WORLD:** the environment alone; actor and living targets hidden; original game remains in background state.
- **SHAPE:** same playable actor in a flat dark material on a light backdrop; form and negative space can be judged without material appeal.
- **PLAY:** restored original game scene and target objects with live combat, camera, Thumb, native Resonance, RouteOS dash, phases, persistent record and recovery.

Each pass remains one real WebGL scene, not a separate miniature or fake screen. A VISGUIDE labelled comparison bar preserves direct user access to all four.

## Actually new actor identity
**EMBER KITE · The Sky Courier:** a fox/glider-like **four-legged** actor with broad two-surface sail-wings, upright ears, faceted muzzle/eyes, independently pivoted four paws, a long forked feather tail, a fluttering asymmetric courier scarf and separate flight movement. Materials are warm ivory/ember/navy/gold, deliberately contrasted against the Vale's teal/cold stone world. Authored geometric form is compiled through the exact pre-existing JM CP10 compiler to Three.js geometry; UI/lights/decorations use Three.js where appropriate.

**No claim of perfect finished character art.** Stage 1 is to prove a genuinely distinct legible silhouette, actor/world separation, and actor-specific motion. Acceptance belongs to the owner and device context.
