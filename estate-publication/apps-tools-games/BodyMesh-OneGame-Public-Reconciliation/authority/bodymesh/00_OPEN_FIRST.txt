JM BODYMESH
Independent-Body Compilation, Backup and Recoverable Mesh Architecture
Publication Edition v1.0 | 3 August 2026

OPEN FIRST

BodyMesh is a separately useful JM Estate body for finding, packetising, selecting, relating, adapting, mounting, testing and recovering independently governed bodies.

FOUNDING LAW
Mesh does not mean merge. Compilation does not erase composition.

PURPOSE
- independent estate backup;
- common-body compilation;
- source regrouping;
- typed relationships between bodies;
- reversible mounts and recoverable separation.

BOUNDARY
BodyMesh does not replace Estate Compass, First Stage, Estate Head, Library, GitHub, Wisebase, chats or Projects. It consumes and compiles governed source without silently taking ownership of it.

ONEGAME RELATIONSHIP
OneGame is an operational consumer and contact-proof bridge. It may turn selected BodyMesh material into entities, scenes, playable loops, phone-fit checks and export/reload tests. OneGame does not define or crown the whole mesh.
