# BUILT ENOUGH: BODY RETURN v0.2 — Formal Specification

## Governing statement

> I have built enough. Now something needs to come back into my life.

## Core law

> Numbers guide the shape. The body sets the boundary. The moment creates the opening. Use supplies the proof.

## Product purpose

BODY RETURN turns ordinary life events into small, personal movement opportunities while also supporting fuller structured sessions.

## Two action scales

1. **Session route** — a multi-movement routine generated from symbolic pattern plus current capacity.
2. **Moment return** — a tiny movement generated from the current situation, available environment and current capacity.

## Moment engine

Input:

- current trigger;
- available environment;
- profile and stated capacity;
- symbolic signature;
- previous receipts.

Output:

- movement lane;
- variation;
- small dose;
- explanation of how the route was selected;
- an option to swap, adapt or use an intention-only version.

## Current triggers

- starting something;
- finishing something;
- feeling good;
- frustration;
- waiting;
- sitting too long.

## Receipt law

A generated suggestion is not proof. A completed or declined action, recorded with response data, creates the receipt.

## Claim separation

- **Symbolic:** pattern and meaning layer.
- **Personal:** what happened for this user under these conditions.
- **Guideline:** broad external context, not diagnosis.
- **Generated:** app-created suggestion that the user may accept, adapt, swap or decline.

## Safety boundary

The app uses stated comfortable capacity and deliberately small fractions for moment returns. It is not a clinical prescription. Pain or worrying symptoms should stop the route and may require appropriate professional advice.

## Reminder boundary

v0.2 uses local timers and optional browser notifications. It does not claim reliable closed-app push delivery.
