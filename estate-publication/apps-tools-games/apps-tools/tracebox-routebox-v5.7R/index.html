Calibration engine
v3 lock: compare predicted state with actual result. Match preserves route. Drift inspects cause. Break repairs model or material route.
Match
Preserve route. Save as reliable until material reality teaches otherwise.
Drift
Inspect Space, Time, Height, Gate, Operator, Material, Signal, Meaning, Memory, or Reset.
Break
Repair model or material route. Do not crown. Do not discard the learning.
Keeper

Public-safe pitch
TraceBox / RouteBox is a translucent route-state model. It asks: what entered, what route shaped it, what state changed, and can we recover the meaning? It does not claim total hidden knowledge. It reveals enough of the route to test, correct, reset, reroute, and act.
