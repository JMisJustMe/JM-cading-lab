# FUTARIZED v3.1 — Combat Flow

Part I remains the Living Body Refit.

Part II is updated to Futarian Field v0.2.

## Touch combat

- Left half drag: move.
- Right half hold/drag: aim and continuously fire.
- Auto-fire is ON by default and targets the nearest active threat while the player moves.
- AUTO: ON/OFF toggles automatic targeting.
- HOLD FIRE is the backup sustained-fire control.
- DASH and TRAIT remain separate actions.

## Combat rebalance

- Lower basic-shot energy cost.
- Faster energy recovery.
- Faster shot cadence and projectile speed.
- LIGHT improves basic shot damage.
- SPLIT now fires a three-shot spread.
- THROUGH gains stronger piercing.
- BESIDE companion fires more regularly.
