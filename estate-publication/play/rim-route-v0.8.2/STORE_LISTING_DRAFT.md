## Full description
JM Rim Route is a portrait-first basketball game built around live ball contact, movement and timing.

Start in Practice and learn the route from dribble to release. Move into VS AI for live offence and defence, or hand the phone across in Local 2P for pass-and-play rounds on one device.

Gameplay includes:
- live dribbling and ball contact;
- timed shot release and balance;
- passing, cutting, screens and relocation;
- contests, steals and defender pressure;
- Practice, VS AI and Local 2P modes;
- playable cards including Dash, Aim+, Momentum and Shield;
- persistent upgrades such as Fast Feet, Shooter's Eye and Pass Pulse;
- local gameplay state stored on the device.

JM Rim Route is deliberately local-first. This v0.8.2 release does not claim online PvP, accounts, cloud saves, advertising or analytics.
