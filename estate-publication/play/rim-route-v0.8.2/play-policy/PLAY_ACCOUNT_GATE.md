# JM Rim Route — owner-only Play account gate

Everything in this pack is intended to be completed before paying/registering wherever possible.

The remaining account-bound steps are intentionally not performed here:
- create/confirm the Google Play Console developer account;
- pay Google's one-time developer registration fee;
- complete legal identity/contact verification;
- complete real-Android-device verification if Google requires it for the new personal account;
- create the Play app record using the final package identity;
- enable Play App Signing and create/store the permanent owner upload key using an owner-controlled secret location;
- upload the exact release AAB;
- provide the final public privacy-policy URL/support contact;
- select target audience, countries/regions, price and other owner/publisher choices;
- enrol testers and complete the required closed-test period;
- apply for production access and submit for review.

## Signing law
The CI key in this prep route is **proof-only and disposable**. It must not become the permanent Play upload/update identity.
