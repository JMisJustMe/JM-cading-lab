# JM Rim Route — closed-test pack

## Account rule to satisfy
For a newly created personal Play developer account subject to Google's current production-access rule, keep **at least 12 testers continuously opted in for at least 14 days** before applying for production access.

## Tester instruction
Install only from the Play closed-test link supplied by the developer. Keep the test opted-in for the full required period unless the developer explicitly ends the test.

Run these checks at least once:
1. Install and launch the game.
2. Complete Practice and make at least three baskets.
3. Play VS AI on offence and defence.
4. Test dribble, pass, shot release, contest and steal controls.
5. Use at least two cards/upgrades if available during the run.
6. Enter Local 2P and complete a pass-the-phone exchange.
7. Close the app fully, reopen it and check that expected local progression/state returns.
8. Toggle sound and pause/resume.
9. Open the in-app Privacy policy from the app menu and return to the game.
10. Report any cut-off controls, overlap, unreadable text, frozen input, unexpected network prompt or crash.
