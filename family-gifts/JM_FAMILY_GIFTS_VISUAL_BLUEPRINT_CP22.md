# JM FAMILY GIFTS — Visual and Behaviour Blueprint · CP22
_29 October 2026 · Original, sovereign JM source families · Candidate design reference_

## Authority and purpose
This blueprint describes improvements to the **actual two existing gifts**, not a third product, merged front door or replacement design.
- **THIRD COURT**: `family-gifts/third-court-preview/cp11-ball-contact-candidate.html`
- **JM Scripture Companion**: `family-gifts/scripture-companion/index.html`
- The two applications retain independent identity, inputs, saved data, recoverability and publication routes.
- Treat owner-device visuals/feel as the final acceptance gate. A rendered screenshot is evidence of a viewport, not of identical use on all devices.

## A. THIRD COURT — three-dimensional athlete expression
**Current inheritance**: CP08 real WebGL geometry + CPU 2.5D + Classic, CP10 contact defence, CP11 ball-contact projection, CP20 rim-origin misses, CP21 gravity/ground rebounds.
**CP22 contact**: one role-form table informs both actual footwork and native/2.5D visual bodies.

| Player role | Body language / silhouette | Physical feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Floor General | Narrower torso, faster full stride | Responsive acceleration and braking |
| Wing Shooter | Balanced frame, measured stride | Mid-weight acceleration / release movements |
| Paint Anchor | Broad frame, compact stance | Heavy acceleration and braking, wider contact presence |

**Camera contract**
1. TRUE 3D: spatial geometry, correct court depth, limbs and ball share physical state.
2. Broadcast/CPU 2.5D: projected court and moving athlete bodies are not flattened back to colour tokens.
3. Classic overhead: deliberately preserved fallback; no loss of playable control.

**Next visual layers**, additive and independently testable: off-ball footwork (defence, cuts), role-identifiable hair/gear/outlines, silhouette separation even without colour, hoop / glass spatial contact, better basketball hand-to-ball pose, stable frame rate, accessible controls and on-screen feedback. Never re-skin a shared generic athlete and call it six new bodies.

**Acceptance**: actual position change, turn/pivot telemetry, three distinct geometric widths, true ball bounce; Chromium at 390/1280, owner phone and laptop feel, native camera fallback preserved; no unproven full-physics claim.

## B. MUM'S SCRIPTURE COMPANION — welcoming reading-room visual language
**Identity**: a warm, dignified, private study aid. NOT a replacement for the New World Translation nor an embedded offline copy of it. The original Bible catalogue selects chapters and links to official JW.org text and study materials; notes and personal progress are local JM-created data.

**Reading hierarchy / wave of attention**:
Welcome back → resume reference → chapter navigation → opening official text → personal reflection → save/backup. Reading is one calm route; exploration is discoverable but does not overwhelm the home.
- **Phone (~390px)**: one reading card per row, comfortable tap targets, persistent five existing bottom sections, no horizontally clipped notes.
- **Laptop (~1280px)**: contained central reading column, no novel dashboard, larger white space not stretched text, keyboard focus visible.
- **Fold/tablet**: continuous responsive reflow; no hard device-only content branches.
- Book picker has two labeled sections, Hebrew-Aramaic (39) and Christian Greek (27), preserving all 66 original values and 1,189 total chapter routes.
- A small on-screen catalogue summary distinguishes _chapter navigation_ from _online Scripture text_.

**Interaction state contract**
- Read and unread must be reversible.
- Selecting a study subject may persist as a separate UI preference, without altering the private backup schema.
- Existing notes, unfinished drafts, bookmarks, reading lists and imported backups must remain unchanged by appearance edits.
- Avoid storing copyrighted Bible text, importing off-site page content, or advertising official JW endorsement.
- Never silently overwrite a note; keep local backup, export and recovery flows accessible.
- External Study Edition links are official sources, not a claim every verse and study annotation is cached.

**Next visual layers to audition** (do not promote on a mockup): chapter-and-book shelf preview, softer subject route cards, large-text reading mode, optional high-contrast refinement, accessible focus, a backup safety indicator. Owner decides aesthetic fit after using it.

## CP22 gates / change control
- All changes are append-only or tightly scoped modifications in the original live sources; no replacement of the original birthday edition.
- CI must run both original Chromium runtime suites at **390px and 1280px**. Assert six-role baseline, real pivots, rebound contact, complete Scripture catalogue, route boundaries, and note/backup regressions.
- Release / deploy status, external source editability and owner-device judgement are separate; **No Ding No Claim**.
- If a check fails, keep the proven preceding version; fix the test against real events, not timer guesses. No throwaway download packs or dead descendants.

**Design advancement law**: Inherit working geometry, movement, access, identity and data. Prototype forward inside the sovereign body. Compare before promote. Integration is not replacement.
