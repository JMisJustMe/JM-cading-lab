Private backup & device transfer

Move notes, saved passages and reading progress between your devices using your own backup file. Nothing is uploaded by this app; changes do not sync automatically.

Add keeps this device's notes, merges reading progress, skips duplicates and protects different versions of a note. Imported unfinished drafts are kept as recovered notes. Replace overwrites the current device after explicit confirmation: download its backup first.
