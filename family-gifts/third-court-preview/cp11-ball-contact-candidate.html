THE RUN.

CAMERA: perspective Broadcast or overhead Classic. Both preserve the same game physics and possession. CALL CUT: send an off-ball teammate towards the hoop (C on keyboard). MOVE: drag the left joystick or use WASD / arrows. SHOOT: hold and release in the green timing window. PASS: aim with the stick to choose a teammate; on defence, PASS switches your defender. DRIVE / STEAL: burst towards the hoop with the ball, or press a ball-carrier when defending.

Three completed team passes charge a Crown Run. A green-release basket can earn four points. Every attack runs on a possession clock: 18 seconds in the full match, 12 in Final Minute. Shoot before zero! Passing does not reset the clock. CALL CUT: use the CUT control or C key to send a teammate towards the rim; aim and pass before the defender recovers.

CONTROLLER · CP29: Left stick moves the actual Floor General, Wing Shooter or Paint Anchor. Hold/release A to shoot on original timing; B passes or switches defender; X drives or steals; Y calls a cut; LB sets a screen; hold RB to guard; Back changes camera; Start tips off or pauses/resumes. All actions share the original court, physics, fatigue and possession rules.
