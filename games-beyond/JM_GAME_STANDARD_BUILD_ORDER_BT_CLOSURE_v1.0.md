## Founding laws brought together

1. **NO RESTART.** Recover the strongest existing body and donors before rebuilding.
2. **CORE BEFORE ORNAMENT.** The game must actually play before decorative complexity is allowed to become authority.
3. **PROTECT PLAYFIELD BEFORE SETUP CHROME.** Fit and contact are structural, not late polish.
4. **AUTHOR THE THINGS THE PLAYER TOUCHES.** Primary game objects deserve authored visual bodies before backgrounds are asked to carry identity.
5. **ONE STATE MAY HAVE MULTIPLE PRESENTATIONS.** Presentation may express authority but must not silently become a second engine.
6. **PROGRESSION MUST BECOME PERCEIVABLE CONSEQUENCE.** Rank/stage change should alter the world where appropriate, not only text or badges.
7. **RICHNESS MUST CARRY MEANING.** Material, lighting and depth exist to strengthen identity, focus or state meaning, not ornament soup.
8. **INTERACTION MUST PRODUCE PERCEIVABLE CONSEQUENCE.** Visible-state feedback may express already-authoritative state but must not invent or leak hidden state.
9. **THE GAME DOES NOT GRADE ITSELF.** Independent Estate/runtime standards must test the rendered body.
10. **DEEPEN THE PRODUCT, NOT THE MENU COUNT.** Persistence, review, teaching, AI, continuity and mastery are game-specific depth organs, not compulsory furniture.
11. **CURRENT AUTHORITY BEATS REMEMBERED AUTHORITY.** Mature stages audit against the current Estate visual/crown standard.
12. **REAL-VIEWPORT STRUCTURAL PROOF ≠ AESTHETIC CROWN.** Automated proof and owner judgement remain distinct.
13. **ZERO-BEHIND OR RECONCILE.** Never force-promote a stale descendant over current authority.
14. **SOURCE → OPEN_FIRST → ESTATE MOUNT → LIVE PROOF → DIRECT DOOR.** Public contact is proved through the Estate rail, not assumed from source success.
15. **LIVE HTTP CONTACT ≠ COLD DING.** Owner physical-device contact remains sovereign.
16. **BUILD ONCE → LEARN ONCE → DONATE MECHANISM → NEW BODY STARTS HIGHER.** Successful learning must raise the next body's starting floor.

## Identity and mesh safeguards

**STEAL THE MACHINERY, NOT THE FURNITURE.**  
**MESH ≠ MERGE.**

PUKA is the proof lineage for the order, not the identity template for later games. A future game may inherit proved donors and begin higher, but it may not silently skip the corresponding proof gate. Different games may share organs, standards and tests while retaining sovereign mechanics, world identity and presentation language.
