# Games&Beyond — OWNER ACCESS FIRST v1

**Status:** GOVERNANCE CORRECTION / RE-ENTRY LAW

## Root law

> **IMPERFECTION IS A REASON FOR CONTACT, NOT EXILE.**

Operational keeper:

> **RE-ENTRY BEFORE PERFECTION.**

## Separation

**STANDARD decides what may be claimed.**  
**PUBLICATION decides what may be presented outwardly.**  
**OWNER ACCESS decides whether JM can re-enter an owned body.**

These are separate rails. A game may be prototype, developing, diagnostic, resting, HOLD, current executable, Estate-standard, public, or crowned without losing owner re-entry.

## Games&Beyond rule

1. No game is hidden merely because it is unfinished.
2. Every known game/body remains represented in the Whole Games Estate catalog.
3. Public/release filters may affect outward presentation, never owner custody or recoverability.
4. An unavailable public carrier must not be interpreted as an unavailable owned body.
5. Re-entry uses the strongest exact current/canonical body already owned; **SEARCH BEFORE RECOVERY. RECOVER BEFORE REBUILD.**
6. Historical heads remain reachable as lineage where they matter, but do not displace the current re-entry head.
7. HOLD means a claim/identity/authority question is unresolved; it does not mean access is denied.
8. Owner contact may promote, correct, preserve, branch, or retire a body. Lack of contact must not silently bury it.

## Development consequence

A body cannot earn stronger quality through contact if the system removes it from practical contact because it has not already earned stronger quality. Therefore quality gating must never create a circular access gate.

**ACCESS → CONTACT → OBSERVE → CORRECT → PRESERVE → ADVANCE/CROWN WHEN EARNED.**

## Boundary

Owner access does not automatically publish private source, grant public release status, or fabricate a completion crown. Public showcase and owner workbench are related but distinct surfaces.

## Keeper

> **THE CROWN CAN WAIT. THE BODY CANNOT DISAPPEAR.**
