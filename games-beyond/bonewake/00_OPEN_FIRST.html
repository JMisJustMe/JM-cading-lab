HUMANIMALS · FIRST WAKE · QUICK START

Wake the arena in four moves.

  1. OPTIONAL: tap WAKE POOL to weight creatures, then choose a MOMENT LEADER. Your choices influence the reel; they don't dictate it.
  2. HOLD & RELEASE the WAKE LEVER. A Dropwake creature appears on the board.
  3. IMPORTANT: touch that Dropwake inside the central game board, drag backwards to aim, then release your finger to flick it. The lever alone does not launch.
  4. After launch, press LEFT / RIGHT REFLEX (or the lower board halves) to keep bodies alive and wake fossil routes. Break the Enemy Core.

Try it once; your arena changes through actual contact. You can reopen this guide anytime.
