Three fighters start on the floor, two support from the yard and one is flexible. Select two fighters. Keep BOTH for a shared move, or tap A and B to give each one its own action and tap/flick destination. Press COMMIT PAIR; both fighters act together as the rival crew issues its own two orders. Yard fighters shield or intervene; the flex can deploy as a fourth floor fighter. Capture the two glowing relay zones to gain tactical points.
