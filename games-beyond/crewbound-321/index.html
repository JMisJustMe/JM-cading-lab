HOW TO PLAY · 3+2+1

Three fighters start on the floor, two support from the yard and one is flexible. Tap two character cards, choose COMBO, ATTACK, GUARD, MOVE or DEPLOY, then tap or flick a destination on the field. Press COMMIT PAIR. Two rival orders resolve at the same time. All floor fighters continue moving and defending even between your orders. Yard characters can shield and assist; the flex character can deploy as a fourth floor fighter. Dice is optional.
