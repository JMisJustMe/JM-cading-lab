HOW TO PLAY · 3+2+1

Three fighters start on the floor, two support from the yard and one is flexible. Before the first go, tap ARRANGE STARTING SIX and then two fighters with different roles to exchange their starting positions. Tap DONE ARRANGING before issuing combat commands. Your six identities remain the same; only their first positions change. Select two fighters. Keep BOTH for a shared move, or tap A and B to give each one its own action and tap/flick destination. Press COMMIT PAIR; both fighters act together as the rival crew issues its own two orders. All floor fighters continue moving and defending between your orders. Each character has its own combat kit and periodically triggers a signature manoeuvre when contact is made. Yard fighters shield or intervene; the flex can deploy as a fourth floor fighter. Capture the two glowing relay zones to gain tactical points. Win by disabling the rival crew or leading on remaining crew health plus relay points at the three-minute limit. Dice is optional.
