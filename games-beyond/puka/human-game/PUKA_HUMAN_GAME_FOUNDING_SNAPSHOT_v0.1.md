## Founding purpose

PUKA was not conceived as only a poker simulator. The game lane and the human-game lane were intended to develop together.

Poker was chosen first because it gives repeated contact with:

- uncertainty;
- incomplete information;
- bluffing and counter-bluffing;
- signalling and belief;
- pressure and response to pressure;
- confidence and apparent confidence;
- risk and restraint;
- pattern recognition;
- decision consequence;
- revision after contact.

The human-game lane therefore belongs inside PUKA's product intelligence rather than being reduced to decorative poker tips.

## Founding route

**CONTACT -> OBSERVATION -> CLASSIFY WHAT IS ACTUALLY VISIBLE -> INTERPRETATION -> CALIBRATED READ -> DECISION -> CONSEQUENCE -> TRACE -> REVIEW -> CORRECT / RETAIN / WEAKEN THE READ**

Compressed:

**SEE -> SEPARATE -> READ -> TEST -> TRACE -> CORRECT.**

## Three sovereign layers

### 1. Game truth

Mechanical authority remains sovereign:

- cards and board state;
- legal actions;
- stack/pot/bet state;
- outcome and showdown;
- progression/history/review;
- rules and evaluator truth.

Psychology may never rewrite mechanical truth.

### 2. Observation / behavioural trace

The human-game layer may use contact that is actually available to the player or lawfully recorded by the game, such as:

- check / call / fold / raise;
- deliberate raise sizing;
- repeated pressure or restraint patterns;
- street and position context;
- visible board and price context;
- hand history and decision review;
- consequences of prior decisions.

Future descendants may add other lawful observable traces, but must name their source.

### 3. Interpretation / behavioural read

Interpretations may include bounded hypotheses such as:

- aggression / passivity tendency;
- pressure response;
- apparent bluff frequency;
- risk tolerance in a defined game context;
- consistency / inconsistency across comparable situations;
- possible signalling strategy.

These are **reads**, not hidden facts, diagnoses, motives or identity claims.

## Founding laws

1. **Observation != interpretation != fact.**
2. **A behavioural pattern earns a read, not a mind-reading licence.**
3. **A read must remain corrigible by later contact.**
4. **Action under uncertainty does not prove motive, confidence, honesty or deception by itself.**
5. **Repeated contact may strengthen or weaken a bounded tendency claim; it does not automatically establish a stable personality trait.**
6. **Game truth outranks psychology inference.**
7. **Visible-card estimates remain estimates; they may not inspect or imply knowledge of hidden House cards.**
8. **Unrevealed information remains unrevealed after fold/history/review.**
9. **The psychology layer may learn from traces; it may not become a second poker engine or hidden-state authority.**
10. **Teaching should distinguish what happened, what may explain it, and what remains unknown.**
11. **The Human Game is part of PUKA's mechanics/product intelligence, not merely flavour text.**
12. **Future card-game descendants may reuse the reasoning grammar without inheriting poker furniture or PUKA identity.**
13. **No real-world behavioural case becomes canonical evidence merely because it inspired the design.**
14. **Real-world case discussion must preserve statement / event / inference separation and must not be silently converted into player profiling or diagnosis.**

## Already-present compatible organs in PUKA v0.13A

The current PUKA body already preserves part of this founding lane through:

- `Observation != interpretation != fact` as a registered law;
- `Estimate != hidden fact`;
- hidden House-card secrecy;
- visible-card equity against an unknown range;
- active-hand persistence;
- latest-hand history;
- decision review;
- deliberate raise sizing;
- mastery / lesson progression;
- bounded presentation organs that may express visible state but may not duplicate hidden authority.

These organs are not the completed Human Game engine. They are lawful foundations the next descendant must retain or explicitly supersede with stronger proved mechanisms.

## What remains deliberately open

Later descendants may deepen:

- player tendency models;
- confidence/calibration of behavioural reads;
- bluff / counter-bluff teaching;
- pressure-response modelling;
- context-sensitive opponent modelling;
- explanation / Why layers;
- comparative pattern windows;
- contradiction / inconsistency handling;
- uncertainty language;
- human-readable evidence trails;
- cross-card-game transfer;
- optional real-world reasoning exercises kept separate from game-player profiling.

No specific implementation is crowned by this snapshot.

## Forward law

Future work must **descend from this snapshot rather than restart beneath it**.

A stronger descendant may add, refine, split or replace mechanisms when contact earns it, but it must preserve the founding distinctions unless it explicitly records why a distinction has been superseded.

**DO NOT TURN A READ INTO A FACT JUST BECAUSE IT BECOMES USEFUL.**
