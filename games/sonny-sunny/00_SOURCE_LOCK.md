## Why the accidental word remains valuable

The accidental term reveals two related but distinct game-quality routes.

### Sonny

**Sonny** means the game is tested against Theodore’s son as a real recipient and tester:

- the kinds of games he actually enjoys;
- the technologies, effects and interaction styles that attract him;
- the UI density, speed and clarity he responds to;
- whether the game feels exciting before its architecture is explained;
- whether he chooses to continue, retry, explore and show another person;
- what he ignores, misunderstands, exploits or finds boring.

Sonny must not become a generic stereotype of what children like. It is an observed recipient route grounded in Theodore’s son’s actual contact, choices and playtest traces.

### Sunny

**Sunny** means JMLogic is visibly lit rather than hidden behind dull tokens, menus or unexplained variables:

- action is readable before and during contact;
- aim, charge, danger, role and consequence are visible;
- state change alters the field clearly;
- feedback has energy, rhythm and visual life;
- the UI protects play while making the next meaningful action obvious;
- Visualang carries real game logic through shape, space, colour, motion, contact, transformation and trace.

Sunny does not require a cheerful story or bright colour palette. It requires logical illumination and lively readable consequence.

## Flagship application

The current flagship direction is one deep 2-v-2 PvP action game derived from FOURFOLD, T-Boys and SS lineages:

- two characters genuinely in use per side;
- Flick Clash as the physical drag → aim → release heart;
- Clash Flow as one-hand semi-assisted enactment;
- Clash Manual as direct two-thumb enactment;
- meaningful attacks, damage, defence, assists, tags, arena consequence and emergent battle story;
- no shallow engine-demo loop accepted as game-quality proof;
- no new slice beginning below the strongest relevant proven quality equivalents.

The game must be built as a game first, then reusable organs may be extracted into the engine after transfer is proven through another game.

## Game Studio route

The flagship is presently classified as a **2D action PvP browser game**.

Default carrier route:

1. JM coding bodies author the game first.
2. Phaser + TypeScript + Vite carry the 2D runtime.
3. DOM carries readable HUD, menus and tactical UI.
4. A dedicated sprite/effects pipeline carries character animation, impact and arena feedback.
5. Browser and phone playtests judge feel, clarity, replay and recipient contact.

Phaser is a carrier, not the authority. Conventional code must implement the game body rather than replace it.

## Sonny / Sunny gates

A slice does not pass merely because it launches or records collisions.

It must show evidence that:

- its declared quality floor was restored or exceeded;
- the first playable seconds communicate what is exciting;
- attacks have satisfying anticipation, contact and consequence;
- character and partner roles are visibly different;
- the three manners preserve depth rather than reducing it;
- the UI does not smother the playfield;
- a real recipient can understand and enjoy it without architecture narration;
- traces capture what happened without interrupting play;
- Theodore’s son’s observed response is preserved as evidence rather than guessed in advance.
