## Combat body

**MOVE → FACE → ENGAGE → ATTACK → DEFEND → EVADE → COUNTER → ASSIST → TAG → CHAIN → KNOCKDOWN → RECOVER → PRESSURE → FINISH → TRACE**

- Clash Manual is first.
- Health and Guard/Poise are separate.
- Every damaging move has startup, active contact, recovery, Health damage, Poise damage, range, width, knockback and a named visual route.
- Collision without an authored active move creates no damage.
- Self-damage exists only when a named move explicitly declares and displays it; no v0.1 move does.
- Assist selects a living opponent and never an ally.
- Tag, Duo Surge and Last Body keep the partner operationally present.
- Aiming belongs inside skills and selected combat routes; it is not the whole fight.
