# T-Boys Direct-Touch Combat Grammar v1.0

**State:** ACTIVE INPUT FLOOR / BUTTONS ARE BACKUP

## Core law

> **The touchscreen is the combat surface. Buttons remain optional equivalents, not the primary carrier.**

## Primary two-thumb field

### Left contact — locomotion and facing

- Touch the arena near the active fighter to summon a temporary contact pad under the thumb.
- Drag to move; distance controls speed.
- Curving the thumb route curves movement and facing.
- Releasing stops or settles according to fighter momentum.
- Double-flick creates evade in the flick direction.

The pad is contextual and disappears after release. No permanent joystick is required.

### Right contact — combat shaping

Touch/hold in the combat half of the arena to open a translucent action field around the contact point.

- **Short directional flick:** Quick attack.
- **Longer committed pull and release:** Heavy attack.
- **Hold, shape aim route, release:** Skill.
- **Hold without crossing attack threshold:** Guard.
- **Fast lateral or backward cut:** Evade.
- **Swipe from partner portrait/edge marker into the field:** Assist route.
- **Tap or drag the partner marker onto the active route:** Tag / handoff.
- **Two-contact convergence when momentum is full:** Duo action.

## Targeting

- Tap a rival to focus them without freezing free movement.
- Drag through a rival to bind an authored attack route.
- Aim lines originate from the fighter or attack tool, not from a detached UI button.
- Friendly bodies are excluded from hostile target resolution.
- Assist routes visibly show ally avoidance, interception and destination before commitment.

## Visualang contact states

- Touch anchor = pale contact bloom.
- Quick threshold = narrow live wedge.
- Heavy threshold = widening pressure cone.
- Skill route = fighter-specific line, curve, arc, tether or area.
- Guard = closing shell around fighter stance.
- Evade = split afterimage route.
- Assist = partner-coloured joining line.
- Tag = crossing handoff trace.

## Backup controls

A collapsible **BUTTON BACKUP** rail may expose Quick, Heavy, Skill, Guard, Evade, Assist and Tag for accessibility, testing and preference. It must remain collapsed by default during normal play and may not cover the lower-middle playfield.

## Translation requirement

Direct touch and button backup must call the same authored move/state contracts. Neither route may become a reduced or separate combat game.
