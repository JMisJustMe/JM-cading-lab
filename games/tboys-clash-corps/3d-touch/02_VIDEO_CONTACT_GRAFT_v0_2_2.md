## Video findings

- The 3D graphical direction is an acceptable beginning floor.
- Movement still read as a floating controller moving a model rather than a fighter carrying deliberate combat position.
- Screen movement and world movement needed a stronger camera-relative relationship.
- Attacking auto-engaged and closed distance too aggressively.
- Fighters collapsed into overlapping body clusters instead of maintaining readable fighting range.
- Attack results were clearer in bars than in bodies.
- AI decisions and damage arrived too quickly for readable answer and recovery.
- The lower instruction panel occupied active play space throughout contact.

- long-drag skill;
- drag-away evade;
- wider fighter separation;
- attack root movement stops before crossing the defender;
- reduced damage and poise throughput;
- post-hit grace window;

- tutorial auto-hide.
