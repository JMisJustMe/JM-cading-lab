# JM Current-Best Quality Floor — Governing Law v1.0

**Authority:** Theodore Benjamin Scott / JM / JMISJUSTME  
**State:** ESTATE-WIDE HARD BUILD GATE  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

## Core law

> **Nothing starts below Theodore's strongest proven quality equivalent.**

A new body, branch, game, app, tool, carrier, prototype, vertical slice or experiment must begin at the strongest relevant quality floor already demonstrated anywhere accessible in the JM estate.

`New` does not mean `basic`.

`Prototype` does not mean `lower quality`.

`Vertical slice` does not mean `temporary regression`.

`Different carrier` does not mean `forget proven gameplay, visual, interaction, story, UI or coding quality`.

## Required route

**MENTION / AIM**  
→ **RECALL RELEVANT BODIES**  
→ **LOCATE STRONGEST CURRENT-BEST EQUIVALENT**  
→ **DECLARE THE QUALITY FLOOR**  
→ **RESTORE ITS PROVEN CAPABILITIES**  
→ **BUILD THE NEW BODY AT OR ABOVE THAT FLOOR**  
→ **TEST DIFFERENCE AND GAIN**  
→ **1% CRITIQUE OR 101% EXTENSION**  
→ **TRACE / RECEIPT / DING**

No implementation may begin before the relevant quality floor is named.

## What the quality floor includes

The floor is not only code completion. It includes every relevant proven dimension:

- gameplay depth;
- action and consequence;
- story or emergent battle meaning;
- character identity;
- visual quality and Visualang readability;
- control feel;
- UI clarity;
- animation and effects;
- sound response where available;
- coding-body authority;
- performance;
- accessibility;
- save, trace, export and recovery;
- phone contact;
- evidence and receipts.

A build may simplify scope, cast, arena count or feature breadth while maintaining or increasing quality within the selected scope.

## Scope reduction law

A smaller build is allowed.

A weaker build is not automatically allowed.

For example, a two-character battle may be narrower than FOURFOLD's larger team structure, but its two-character action, visual response, tactics and identity must begin at least equal to the strongest relevant proven equivalents from FOURFOLD, T-Boys, SS and other current-best donor bodies.

## Demonstrated capability law

When a JM body, route or capability has already been demonstrated, later builds must not revert it to hypothetical, absent or technically impossible merely because the current chat or carrier has not yet restored it.

Use:

**RECALL → LOCATE PROOF → IDENTIFY PRESENT ACCESS → RESTORE / ADAPT → OR STATE THE REAL PRESENT LIMIT → PRESERVE THE PROVEN CAPABILITY**

## Current-best is not newest

The newest version does not automatically define the quality floor.

The current-best equivalent is the strongest source-checked and contact-proven body relevant to the new build. Older frozen bodies may remain the quality anchor until a later branch beats them through real contact.

## Prototype classification

A deliberately low-fidelity technical experiment may exist only when:

1. its narrow technical question is declared;
2. it is not presented as the game, app or tool quality body;
3. it does not replace or lower the current-best branch;
4. its visible limitations are stated before delivery;
5. it is stored as a test carrier rather than promoted as the next quality version.

## Failure correction

JM Duo Clash v0.1 violated this law.

It used FOURFOLD, T-Boys and SS as inspiration but did not restore their strongest gameplay, action, character, visual, coding-body and UI quality equivalents before implementation. It therefore remains a regression/test receipt, not a current-best flagship branch.

The corrective flagship build must begin from recovered strongest donors and must not use a crude placeholder slice as its quality foundation.
