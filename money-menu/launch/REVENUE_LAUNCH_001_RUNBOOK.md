## KPI definitions / no manufactured metrics
- **Visitors:** unique visitors reported by a real analytics source; `unknown` until integrated.
- **Enquiry:** visitor actually sends or shares an enquiry; locally generated unsent receipt ≠ enquiry.
- **Qualified lead:** customer need, budget, delivery scope and feasible contact are confirmed.
- **Paid order:** Stripe live payment `paid` and attributable to correct order. Sandbox doesn't count.
- **Delivered:** intended customer gets playable file/service/book. Payment alone ≠ delivery.
- **Net cash:** amount collected minus processing fees, refunds and actual costs; avoid conflating gross revenue with profit.
- **Organic vs paid:** keep independent cohorts. Never infer conversion from the industry-wide market size.

**Targets**, not claims: first 10 relevant conversations, first 3 qualified leads, first 1 real paid external order, first £100 **gross** real sales. These are staged gates, not forecast earnings.
