## What changed

v0.1 proved that the game, contact, trace and export bodies could operate together. v0.2 turns that joined engine into a guided visual authoring body.

The studio operates through:

**INTENTION → BODY → RELATION → FORM → BEHAVIOUR → CONTACT → EVIDENCE → TRACE → PLAY**

The dog demo is not one decorative sticker. Body, head, legs, tail, mouth and sensor remain separately addressable organs. GripCube operations change operational state. FORM, BLUEPRINT and X-FORM share one body. Play produces goal, damage, block and collection consequences. Every committed action leaves a TraceBox receipt.

## Native authoring order

Cading → Kading → JMLogic → FlowTalk → RouteCode → Quadze → RouteOS → GameCore → Visualang PLAY → Living Cursor / Contact Engine → HTML carrier.
