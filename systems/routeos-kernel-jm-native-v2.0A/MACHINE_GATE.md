1. carrier and source hashes pass;
2. adversarial suite passes;
3. inherited v0.2A–v1.9A workflow lineage remains green;
4. v2.0A kernel integration compiles and links;
5. GRUB ISO boots under QEMU;
6. all four v2.0A retained symbols exist exactly;
7. health reports 6/6 PASS;
8. Body 2 faults once and is quarantined;
9. RecoveryBody selects the safe route;
10. Body 1 crosses PermissionGate afterward;
11. live trace decodes into JSON;
12. HTML/SVG observatory renders;
13. Zionfolder contains ELF, ISO, source, receipts, traces, SBOM, checksum ledger and add-ons.

After construction passes, the exact head must be locked and the same gate rerun before a permanent anchor is created.
