What the extension adds

Attention

Not every available signal becomes equally influential. Attention changes what is foregrounded, repeated, rehearsed and acted through.

Suggestion

Words, framing, authority, ritual and context can alter orientation and readiness without becoming the physical event they describe.

Experience

Experience is neither “mere imagination” nor automatic proof of the preferred explanation. It is a real route outcome whose source and mechanism still require separation.

Placebo / nocebo

Inactive ingredient does not mean inactive encounter. Expectation and context may participate alongside active treatment; improvement does not authenticate a practitioner’s theory.

Governed influence

Influence becomes ethically stronger—not weaker—when consent, wording, alternatives, trace and correction remain visible.

Recovery

The endpoint is not obedience or prediction. It is a traceable return route: correction, learning, support and restored participation.
