Stance Stack and Signal Stem

Stance Stack = vertebral column. Signal Stem = spinal cord. Signal Lines = spinal nerves. Soft Spacers = discs.

The spinal cord is neither a passive cable nor a separate miniature person. It is a processing passage.

Senses, readiness and action

Exteroception

Light, sound, touch, temperature, smell and taste.

Proprioception

The body carries an internal map of position through ongoing signal.

Vestibular sense

Balance is coordinated correction, not one isolated sense.

Interoception

Internal signal may become available clearly, vaguely, late, intensely or not consciously at all.

Motor route

Desired result → Action selection → Motor planning → Motor command → Carriage → Muscle recruitment → Movement → Feedback → Correction
Intention participates. The nervous system routes. The body carries. Conditions constrain. Feedback corrects.

Autonomic orientation

The autonomic system participates in heart rate, blood-vessel tone, breathing conditions, digestion, sweating, pupils, glands, bladder and bowel regulation, sexual responses, mobilisation and restoration.

Sympathetic influence.

Vagus route

Major brainstem–organ communication route, not the entire brain–body bond.

Signal, meaning and learned readiness

Signal does not arrive already wearing its final explanation.

Learned readiness

Past contact → Stored route bias → Current cue → Predicted condition → Preparation → Response → Consequence → Updated route

Prediction is not prophecy. It is a readiness structure.
