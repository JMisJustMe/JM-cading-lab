SpalkTalk

Spalk sounds like chalk. SpalkTalk is chalk-like guidance in the moment: formed, visible and useful, but erasable and changeable. It requires holding, doing or marking, like chalk.

Spalk gives the moment a shape, but leaves the shape erasable.

SpelkTalk

The correct term is Spelk, not Speak. Spelk connects to spell and casting. SpelkTalk is casting meaning, signal or route through speech or symbol without requiring the same hand-held chalk/body-marking route.

Spalk marks the board. Spelk casts the route.

Speakuals

Speech-born actuals: spoken symbols, sound-marks or meaning-units that can become signs, rules, code tokens, rituals, commands, lyrics or proof-catches.

Speakuals balance SpalkTalk and SpelkTalk.

EyesOn / EyesOn-Spotcheck

EyesOn is not only visual. It is visual, verbal and listening attention.

EyesOn is active attention-on-contact: seeing, hearing, saying and checking what is actually happening now.

EyesOn-Spotcheck is a fast contact check using sight, listening and verbal confirmation to catch what is visible, audible, missing, drifting, working or ready.

EyesOn sees it, hears it, says it and checks it.
