D. Field Rule

A field is not just a place.

A field is:

where interaction happens
what shapes the interaction
what receives pressure
what responds
what remembers or forgets
what makes the next action different

Example fields:

Game arena — Player, enemy, wall and rule interact; score changes, objects move and win/loss occurs.

Conversation — Speaker, listener, tone and memory interact; meaning and trust change.

AI chat — User, AI, prompt and correction interact; output improves or drifts.

Classroom — Student, teacher, task and pressure interact; understanding grows or collapses.

Body — Breath, stress, posture and attention interact; tightness releases or increases.

Phone world — User, avatar, app realm and signal interact; realm changes and powers unlock.
