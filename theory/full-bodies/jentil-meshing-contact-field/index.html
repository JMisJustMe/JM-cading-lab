Jentil Meshing Contact Field Layer

Identity

Jentil Meshing is the contact-field / relation layer of the JM ecosystem.

It reads how things meet, influence, overlap, resist, route and remain distinct.

Main Function

How do things meet without being flattened into the same thing?

Meshing Is Not Merging

Jentil Meshing does not mean everything becomes one blur. It means contact can create relation, pressure, route and change while preserving individual identity.

Mesh ≠ merge
Contact ≠ collapse
Overlap ≠ sameness
Influence ≠ replacement
Relation ≠ erasure
Similarity ≠ identity

Core Laws

SAME-MARK ≠ SINGLE-FUNCTION
SAME-NAME ≠ SAME-STATE
NO_CONTACT_FIELD = BLIND_ROUTE
CONTACT_FIELD → ROUTE_EFFECT

What It Reads

What is touching what?
What is influencing what?
What pressure appears in the contact?
What route opens because of the meeting?
What remains separate?
What changes state?
What must not be flattened?
What is a true bridge and what is a false merge?

Examples

  • Body and speech can interact without becoming the same thing.
  • Game and engine can support each other without becoming identical.
  • Father and son can form a team without losing individual powers.
  • Science and story can influence a world without science becoming fantasy or fantasy pretending to be science.
  • Branch and hub can synchronise without the branch being swallowed by the hub.
  • Symbol and function can overlap without becoming one function.

Contact Field

A contact field is the live situation where source, signal, body, environment, pressure, memory, tool, branch and output meet.

Without contact-field reading, the route becomes blind.

NO_CONTACT_FIELD = BLIND_ROUTE
CONTACT_FIELD → ROUTE_EFFECT

Keeper Line

Meshing lets elements touch, route and change without killing their difference.
