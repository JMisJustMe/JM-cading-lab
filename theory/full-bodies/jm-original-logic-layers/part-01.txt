A few recurring original logic layers deserve to be added back into the route/trace spine.

1. Pressure

The systems do not just move from input to result. They usually begin when something is under pressure.

Pressure reveals the route.

Stress, confusion, failure, body strain, bad UI, broken meaning, hidden process and silence are not just problems. They expose where a system needs to be built.

2. Field / Environment

Action does not happen in empty space.

The field changes the meaning of the action.

Same action, different arena, different result.

This applies to games, speech, body, tools, story, health, learning and relationships.

3. Support before correction

Do not correct the person or system before giving it a recoverable route.

Support is not weakness. Support is what makes participation possible.

4. Proof-of-feel

The test is not only, “Does it technically work?”

Does it feel usable?
Does it respond?
Does the body understand it?
Does the route make sense under pressure?

That is why a gesture can technically work and still be wrong: it may fail proof-of-feel.

5. Governance / containment

Let things emerge, but do not let emergence hijack the route.

That includes no drift, no accidental merging, no over-expansion, no silent feature creep and no assistant hunch becoming the main build.

6. Translation / bridging

Much of the ecosystem is not making new material from nowhere. It takes what already exists in JM-native form and translates it into a usable public, product, story, game or tool body.

That is BTG logic.

7. Root vs route

Root = why / source / need / origin.
Route = how it travels / becomes usable / reaches form.

The root is preserved while the route changes.

8. Re-entry

Failure is not treated as final.

If the route breaks, re-enter from the last recoverable point.

That law appears in TraceBox, RouteOS, archive work, chat recovery, game builds, body work and story assembly.
