1. Core Definition

TraceBox / RouteBox v5 is a translucent route-state engine.

It models how something enters a system, moves through arranged space-time, meets gates and operators, changes state, produces output, and leaves a trace that can be recovered.

In simple form:

What entered?
What route shaped it?
What state changed?
Can we recover the meaning?

5. The 2⁴: Choice-Structure

The 2⁴ gives the choice-structure.

Formula:

3 × (2 × 2 × 2 × 2)
3 × 2⁴ = 48

Meaning:

3 gives the world-body.
2⁴ gives the choice-structure.
Together they make the route readable.

The four paired gates are:

Gate 1 — Inside / Outside: Is it within the main route or outside it?
Gate 2 — Before / After: Is it before or after the trigger/event?
Gate 3 — Open / Closed: Can the route pass, or is it blocked?
Gate 4 — Input / Output: Is the system receiving or producing?
