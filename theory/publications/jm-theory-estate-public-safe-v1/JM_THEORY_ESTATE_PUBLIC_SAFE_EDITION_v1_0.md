## 4. Second-order strengthening layer

The seven first-order bridges were then treated as bodies and pressure-tested at their adjacent handoffs.

### 1. JM REALITY–CAUSAL ACCOUNTABILITY BRIDGE

A causal claim must answer twice: once to the route that could have produced the change, and again to the reality that can show whether the account of that route is actually true.

### 2. JM MECHANISM–MEANING CONTACT BRIDGE

Meaning can alter later contact, but interpretation must not substitute for the operative route that produced the consequence.

### 3. JM EMBODIED MEANING CARRY BRIDGE

Meaning can enter the whole human without becoming the whole human. Meaning may reorient attention, body-state, action and self-understanding while remaining correctable by contact, capacity and consequence.

### 4. JM AGENCY–RESPONSIBILITY CARRY BRIDGE

Responsibility must meet a real person, not an abstract capability. Capacity, consent, support, authority, participation and recourse remain visible.

### 5. JM RESPONSIBILITY–THROUGH–CHANGE BRIDGE

Responsibility must remember enough history to carry consequence, but contact enough of the present to notice when capacity, consent, authority, dependence or repair have changed.

### 6. JM RECONSTRUCTIVE CONTINUITY BRIDGE

A digital body can remain traceably itself across changing carriers when enough structure, function and lineage survive to reconstruct and test the route. Continuity does not require unchanged substrate or hidden personhood.

### 7. JM REALITY–REFLECTION RETURN BRIDGE

Computational reflection earns authority only to the extent that its representations can return to a reality they did not create, expose their sources and dependencies, survive relevant contact, and accept correction. Coherence can stabilise a route; it cannot close the court.

### Second-order result

The cycle closes back onto Reality Ground without resetting to its earlier state.

> **RETURN TO GROUND ≠ RETURN TO EARLIER STATE.**

The return carries causal accountability, mechanism/meaning distinction, embodied carrying conditions, agency/responsibility boundaries, accountability through change, reconstructive continuity and an anti-self-sealing return route.
