The Brian Greene underdetermination discussion did not crown a specific JM framework. It strengthened a governing distinction already present across EL-Literalism, FTR and the wider Estate.

The ethos must remain subject to its own ethos.
Thing → Encounter → Observation → Record → Model → Use → Consequence → Revision

The solid spine

  • Reality is what it is whether or not we correctly describe it.
  • Human access is routed through sensing, position, memory, language, measurement, interpretation and consequence.
  • A successful model may correspond to reality without becoming identical to reality.
  • Meaning is made through participation, but reality, relationship and result constrain what survives.
  • No title, tradition, authority or usefulness automatically proves final truth.

Three meaning statuses

Authored

Deliberately planted meaning with traceable source intention.

Activated

A deliberately fertile structure later carries a connection that was not necessarily consciously preplanned.

Retrofitted

A later reading is imposed without sufficient trace and then falsely claimed as original intention.

The clean body

Reality is what it is, whether or not we correctly describe it. Human access is routed through encounter, language, interpretation and consequence. Meaning is made through participation, but it is constrained by reality, relationship and result. No model, label, title, tradition or authority becomes the thing it represents, although acting through representations can literally change behaviour and reality. Fundamentals should be applied to oneself first and then carried across domains without granting exemptions or flattening differences. Explanations may remain provisional, but they must never force the route to confirm them. Boundaries can be drawn when earned, but no line inherits permanent authority merely because it already exists.
