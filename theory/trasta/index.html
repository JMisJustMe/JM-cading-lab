Trust the trace.
Not the conclusion.

TraSta handles uncanny overlap, timing, apparent prediction and meaningful coincidence without forcing either dismissal or supernatural coronation.

Preserve the occurrence. Separate the claims. Time-stamp the route. Let repetition—not excitement—decide what grows.
Prior statement / condition → Time-stamped record → External event → Overlap description → Alternative routes → Outcome → Repetition test → Claim boundary

What TraSta is

Trace stance

A disciplined position toward an occurrence: record what happened, what was said beforehand, what changed externally and what was only interpreted afterward.

Not prediction theatre

The goal is not to perform certainty. A correct-seeming prediction may arise through prior information, probability, cue-reading, selection, memory, influence, coincidence or another route.

Cold reading / placebo / nocebo

These are comparison bases and candidate mechanisms—not automatic dismissals and not automatic explanations of the specific case.

Objective overlap

An external event can genuinely overlap with an earlier statement. The overlap itself is preserved even while its mechanism and significance remain open.

Prediction boundary

User position preserved: only God—or “Grater.Odds.Developed”—could truly predict in a knowledge-deprived manner. This is retained as the author’s theological / philosophical boundary, not presented as an empirical demonstration.

TraSta therefore prioritises traceability, route deprivation, replication and honest unknowns over the label “prediction.”
